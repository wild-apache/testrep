Why we make

Not mass-made.
Meaning-made.

There is something different about an object when you know it wasn't pulled from an endless conveyor belt. Every piece here begins as an idea, becomes a mould, gets poured, cured, sanded, checked and finished by hand.

Some pieces come out slightly different from the next. That's not a defect. That's the point.
